# dot.qrware.pl — Manifest

## Why this app exists

**dot** is a minimalist SVG icon generator built around a single, radical idea:
*an icon should live entirely inside its URL.*

Most icon editors force you to create an account, sign in, and store your work
in their database. You end up locked into their platform, their servers, their
downtime, and their data policies.

**dot is the opposite of that.**

### The core principle: stateless, self-contained, permanent

Draw on a 16x16 grid, and the app compresses your icon into a short Base64URL
payload. That payload IS the icon — it carries the complete drawing in the URL
itself. There is:

- **No account.** Nothing to sign up for, nothing to remember.
- **No database.** No server-side state holding your work hostage.
- **No lock-in.** The URL you create today works forever, on any machine, with
  no dependency on dot's servers staying up.

A single line like `/r/eJwrM7Uy...` is a complete, hotlinkable, shareable icon.
Embed it in an `<img>` tag, drop it in a Discord message, use it as a favicon,
or bookmark it — it just works, everywhere, forever.

### Why that matters

Software keeps moving toward centralization and rent-seeking. Every icon you
draw "in the cloud" is a tiny piece of your freedom stored on someone else's
disk. dot pushes back: **your creative output should be yours, encoded in a
portable string you control, not a row in a database you don't.**

It is fast (SVG compiled in <1ms), tiny (no framework bloat), and honest — a
single Bun process, zero dependencies on your side.

### What you can do

- **Create** pixel-perfect icons on a 16x16 grid with line, arc, shapes and fills.
- **Share** them as permanent hotlinks that need no server round-trip to exist.
- **Embed** them anywhere via a tiny web component or a plain `<img>`.
- **Export** to SVG, PNG, WebP, and ICO (favicon).

Because the icon is in the URL, "the cloud" is just a relay — the real copy
lives in your link, and in your hands.
